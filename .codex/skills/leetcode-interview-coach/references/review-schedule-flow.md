# Review Schedule Flow

Use this module after interview readiness assessment.

## Defaults

- `一刷卡壳`: rewrite in 1 day, explain in 3 days, redo in 7 days.
- `提示后完成`: rewrite in 2 days, explain in 7 days.
- `代码有 bug`: rewrite the same day after a break, then again in 3 days.
- `面试表达不熟`: oral explanation in 1 day and 3 days.
- `独立完成`: quick review in 7-14 days.

## Output

Keep review actions concrete:

- `2 天后重写代码`
- `7 天后口述面试版思路`
- `复习 DFS 边界判断模板`
